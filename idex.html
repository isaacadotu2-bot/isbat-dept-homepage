Welcome to the Department of Information Technology at ISBAT University, where we prepare students for careers in software development, networking, and digital systems.

Welcome to the Department

The Department of Information Technology provides practical, hands-on training that connects classroom theory with real-world technology. Our students learn to design, build, and maintain software and systems that meet the needs of organisations today.

Our teaching staff combine academic knowledge with industry experience. Students work on projects in well-equipped laboratories and graduate with the skills employers in Uganda and beyond are looking for.

Programmes Offered

  • Bachelor of Science in Computer Science
  • Bachelor of Science in Information Technology
  • Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering
  • Diploma in Information Technology
